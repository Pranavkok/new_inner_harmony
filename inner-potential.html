Written Before You Were Born

Your fingertips carry a map of the mind.

Fingerprints form in the womb, at the very same time as the brain.

That's why the ridges on your child's fingertips quietly mirror how their brain is wired. It's called dermatoglyphics, the science the DMIT is built on.

A gentle, painless scan turns those patterns into a clear picture of your child's innate potential.
