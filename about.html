My Approach

Where science meets

I started out as a physician, learning to read the body and look for evidence. But working with patients taught me something textbooks couldn't. I realized people carry deep stories that their physical symptoms barely scratch the surface of.

That is why I now combine medical science with holistic healing. I want to help you understand yourself on a deeper level. When we explore your unique thoughts and experiences together, we can find a clearer path to true healing.

My ultimate goal is to offer you a compassionate and safe space. A place where you feel genuinely seen and supported as you grow.
