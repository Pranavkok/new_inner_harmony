Hello, I'm Dr. Gargee

A physician, a healer, a wife & a mother of two

"I don't believe people need fixing. I believe they need a deeper understanding."

I'm Dr. Gargee Gadgil, founder of Inner Harmony — Heal Within. Shine Beyond. For over 28 years of clinical practice, I've had the privilege of guiding thousands of individuals, parents, children and families on their journey toward health, self-understanding, emotional well-being, holistic parenting, financial abundance, good career choices, and personal growth.

Along the way, I came to realise that lasting transformation does not begin by simply changing behaviour — it begins by understanding ourselves more deeply.

My Approach

Where science meets the wisdom of healing

My approach brings together the science of medicine and psychology with the wisdom of holistic healing. Through psychometric assessments, parenting guidance, career counselling, tarot-informed emotional and inner-child healing, and Reiki — to name a few — I help people gain deeper insight into themselves.

When we uncover the thoughts, emotions, beliefs and experiences that shape our lives, we create the opportunity to heal, grow, and move forward with clarity and confidence.

My goal has always been to create a compassionate, non-judgemental space where people feel seen, understood, and empowered to transform their lives.
