INNER HARMONY YOUR NEXT EXPERIMENT
Move without overcommitting

Choose one small way
to gather evidence.

You do not need to decide your whole future. Choose an experiment that teaches you something real.

  • Have one honest conversation with someone doing the work.
  • Try a one-hour beginner lesson, tutorial, or practice project.
  • Notice a task you already do well and name the skill underneath it.
  • Rewrite one role description around contribution, not status.
  • Make a "not for me" list to clarify your boundaries.
My next experiment is… Keep it specific enough to complete within seven days.
I will know more when… What signal, feeling, feedback, or result will you pay attention to?
