INNER HARMONY HOW TO USE THESE CARDS
Connection, not performance

Let curiosity lead
the conversation.

Choose one card at an unhurried moment. Let everyone answer—including the adults. Listen without correcting, teaching, or rushing toward a solution.

1

Choose together

Invite your child to pick a card. Passing is always allowed.

2

Answer with honesty

Simple, age-appropriate honesty helps children see conversation as a shared space.

3

Stay with their answer

Try: "Tell me more," "That makes sense," or "Thank you for telling me."

4

Close with warmth

A hug, a smile, or "I loved hearing that" can be enough.

The goal is to know one another a little better.02
INNER HARMONY CARDS 01—03
01 · NOTICEWhat was one tiny good thing in your day?It can be as small as a tasty bite, a funny sound, or a kind look.
02 · FEELIf your mood had a weather report, what would it say?Sunny, cloudy, stormy, foggy—or something entirely your own.
03 · NEEDWhat helps you feel safe when a day is difficult?Listen for the people, places, words, and routines that matter.
BONUS · FOR GROWN-UPSWhat did your child say that you want to remember?Write one sentence here: ______________________________
Print, cut, and keep somewhere easy to reach.03
INNER HARMONY CARDS 04—06
04 · BELONGWhen do you feel most like yourself?There is no right setting, activity, or kind of company.
05 · IMAGINEIf our family had a secret superpower, what would it be?Kindness? Making people laugh? Solving tricky things together?
06 · REPAIRWhat makes an apology feel real to you?Words, changed actions, a hug, space—or something else?
BONUS · FOR GROWN-UPSWhere can you offer understanding before advice?One moment I want to meet differently: __________________
Connection grows in ordinary moments.04
INNER HARMONY CARDS 07—09
07 · COURAGEWhat is something hard that you handled recently?Notice the effort and the inner quality it took—not only the result.
08 · SUPPORTHow can I make this week feel a little easier for you?Let the answer be practical, playful, emotional, or beautifully small.
09 · JOYWhat should we do more often just because it is fun?Choose one idea you could genuinely make room for.
BONUS · TOGETHEROne small plan we want to make:What: __________________ When: __________________
Children remember how home feels.05
INNER HARMONY CARDS 10—12
10 · BE KNOWNWhat is something you wish grown-ups understood better?You do not have to agree in order to understand.
11 · GRATITUDEWhat is one thing someone in this family does that you appreciate?Be as specific as you can.
12 · HOMEWhat makes our home feel like home to you?A smell, a ritual, a person, a sound, a corner, or a feeling.
KEEP CLOSEYou never have to earn your place in this family.You are loved while learning, growing, trying, resting, and beginning again.
